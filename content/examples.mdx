### `Guided Tour`

A gallery walkthrough that uses scene annotations as tour stops instead of
per-object bindings:

- Each stop carries a title, description, `category`, `images` and `links`
- A saved `cameraView` per stop: the camera flies to stand in front of the artwork
- Previous / next through every stop, or jump to any stop from the list
- Visitors bookmark their own camera views with `savePreset` and `goToPreset` from `useViewerCamera`
- Camera limits through `cameraLimits`: stay inside the room (`insideModel`) or move freely
- Position-only markers, no object binding needed

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/guided-tour) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/guided-tour-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/s/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/guided-tour-demo)

### `Switch Demo`

A focused room-level example where two switches control four lights through app
state:

- Start with exported `objectBindings`.
- Add declarative `effects` to the switch actions.
- Target each light circuit as a binding group instead of listing every light.
- Pass the bindings into `ModelViewer` as React state.
- Pass `useViewerActions().handleAction` to `ModelViewer` and call `runAction` from the panel buttons.
- Use the `useSceneConfig` light controller to synchronize ambient and key-light intensity.
- Attach each point light to its bound bulb object without writing R3F light components.

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/switch-demo) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/switch-room-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/s/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/switch-room-demo)

### `Light Flicker`

An atmospheric lighting example that turns nine static bulb meshes into an
unstable electrical circuit:

- `useObjectBindings` applies the BindingBuilder-exported model setup
- `useSceneConfig` changes the environment, bloom, and vignette by preset
- `useViewerEffects` drives filament, glass, and point-light channels without Three.js access
- `useViewerConnection` shares one viewer-ready callback across camera, effects, and light setup
- Runtime point lights attach to their filament meshes and follow them automatically
- Failing-circuit mode dims every bulb together from one shared signal
- Possessed mode gives every bulb independent dim, flash, and blackout timing
- Lit-up, failing-circuit, and possessed presets change the power behavior
- BindingBuilder-exported bindings and scene configuration drive the model

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/light-flicker) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/light-flicker-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/s/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/light-flicker-demo)

### `Vehicle Configurator`

A product-configurator example that shows how BindingBuilder-exported object
bindings can drive grouped customization flows:

- Binding `group` selectors patch body paint, wheels, trim, and other multi-mesh sets without manual ID arrays
- Built-in `change-color` / `change-material` / `toggle-visibility` actions are
  reinterpreted as real configurator controls
- An aero-kit toggle hides or restores a whole package of parts
- Window tint and headlight state update the same binding map the viewer renders
- Clickable parts and side-panel controls stay in sync through React state

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/vehicle-configurator) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/vehicle-configurator-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/s/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/vehicle-configurator-demo)

### `Engineering Tools`

An inspection workbench that combines the viewer's engineering tools:

- An exploded view with `showExplodeControls`: every bound part slides outward from the model center along its own direction, and dragging back to zero reassembles it exactly (best for static product / CAD models)
- Section cuts on `sceneConfig.section`: one plane, up to six `planes`, or a section `box`, kept in sync with `showSectionTools` through `onSceneConfigChange`
- Angled cuts: any plane can face a custom `normal` instead of the X, Y or Z axis, set from tilt and turn angles
- Isolation with x-ray via `useViewerIsolation`: ghost the rest at a chosen `ghostOpacity`, or hide it
- An object navigator grouped by binding group, with fly-to (`focusObject`) and isolate per part or group, plus the keyboard `showObjectNavigator`
- Distance, angle, area, point-to-plane and circumference measurements with `showMeasureTools`, held as controlled `measurements`
- Model and display units, and calibration against a known length with `calibrateMeasurements`
- CSV export with `measurementsToCsv`

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/engineering-tools) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/engineering-tools-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/p/devbox/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/engineering-tools-demo)

### `Media Export`

Record the motorbike as a video or GIF, without a screen recorder:

- `captureVideo` from `onViewerReady` records only the canvas: no toolbar, panels or cursor
- **Turntable** spins once around the model; **Cinematic** flies a hand-written `cinematic` waypoint path (with `autoPlay: false`, so the camera only moves while recording)
- Export as WebM, MP4 or a looping GIF; formats this browser can't write are disabled
- Short, medium or full-loop length through `duration`; frame rate and GIF width through `fps` and `gifWidth`
- A progress bar while recording, and cancel through an `AbortSignal`
- Preview the result, download it, and keep several recordings side by side

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/media-export) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/media-export-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/p/devbox/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/media-export-demo)

### `Multiple Models`

Compose one scene from several GLB files, with the red room as the primary model:

- `models` entries managed by `useViewerModels`: add, duplicate, hide and remove floor lamps inside the room
- Click a model to make it the active one (`activeModelId`); parked models stay visible
- Each new lamp gets an asset `transform`: `snapAssetToGrid` lines its spot up on a 0.5 m grid and `groundAsset` stands it on the floor
- Move and Scale modes (`moveModeEnabled`, `scaleModeEnabled`) with multi-select (`multiSelectedKeys`) to transform several parts together
- The view gizmo (`showViewGizmo`) snaps the camera to an axis

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/multiple-models) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/multiple-models-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/p/devbox/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/multiple-models-demo)

### `Embedded on a Page`

3D viewers placed in a long, scrolling product page instead of a full-screen app:

- Every viewer shows a `poster` image until its 3D view is drawn
- `loading="lazy"` (the default) starts a viewer only when it comes near the screen; the room further down starts as you scroll to it
- A product range with `reveal="interaction"`: each card keeps its poster and a **View in 3D** button, so no model downloads until a visitor asks
- `pageScroll` lets the mouse wheel and vertical swipes scroll the page over the viewer; Ctrl or ⌘ + wheel zooms
- `reducedMotion` switch on the hero (follow the device, always or never), which stops its auto-rotate
- `webgpu` switch, with the renderer in use shown from `rendererBackend` in `onViewerReady`
- A fullscreen button through `showFullscreenButton`

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/embedded-page) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/embedded-page-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/p/devbox/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/embedded-page-demo)

### `Analytics & Localisation`

Track what visitors do in the viewer, and show its built-in text in their language:

- `onEngagement` hands the page a typed `ViewerEngagementEvent` for each action; the viewer sends nothing anywhere itself
- A live event log with counts per type, the session ID, time since load and Copy JSON; `debugEngagement` also logs to the console
- Car scene: `part-viewed` from the canvas, the Scene Objects panel and the keyboard, and `ar-launch-requested` from Open on phone
- Art gallery scene: `tour-started`, `tour-completed` and `tour-stopped` from the built-in guided tour
- Switching scenes loads a new model, which starts a new analytics session
- One language switch (English, French, Spanish, German) changes everything without reloading the model: `labels` translates the viewer's buttons, tooltips and messages, and the app swaps in its own translated text for the panel, the car's part names (binding `label`) and the tour stops (annotation titles, descriptions and links)

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/analytics-localisation) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/analytics-localisation-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/p/devbox/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/analytics-localisation-demo)

### `Cinematic Tour`

An automatic camera that glides through a space on its own, like a film:

- The `cinematic` prop takes a list of `waypoints` (camera position + look-at
  keyframes)
- The camera follows a smooth Catmull-Rom spline through them at a constant
  on-screen speed, looping seamlessly
- `autoPlay` starts the glide once the model has loaded and framed
- A play / pause control appears in the viewer; grabbing the camera pauses it
- Waypoints are authored visually in BindingBuilder's Cinematic tab and carried
  on `sceneConfig.cinematic`

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/cinematic-tour) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/cinematic-tour-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/s/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/cinematic-tour-demo)

### `Keyframe Sequences`

Keyframed object and camera tracks authored as data on `sceneConfig.sequences`
and driven by `useViewerSequences`. Animation Studio plays clips baked into the
GLB; sequences are authored separately and need no animation in the model:

- An exploded-view reveal that autoplays on load (`autoPlay: true` on the sequence)
- Position, colour and emissive tracks on individual parts, plus a camera track
- `linear`, `ease-in`, `ease-out`, `ease-in-out` and `step` easings side by side
- A looping showroom orbit (`loop: true`)
- A custom picker, play / pause, scrubbable timeline and Stop & restore, with the
  built-in player hidden via `showSequenceControls={false}`

[Live Demo](https://react-immersive.liveroom.dev/examples/reveal-sequences) · [Starter App](https://github.com/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/reveal-sequences-demo) · [Open in CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/p/devbox/github/liveroom-technologies/react-immersive-docs/tree/main/examples/reveal-sequences-demo)

### `Texture Upload / Custom Print`

A custom-apparel demo where shoppers upload artwork, add text and logo decals, and pick a fabric:

- The built-in Upload Artwork action hands the file to `onTextureUpload`
- A real app uploads it to storage and returns a durable URL (here a data URL)
- The returned URL is committed to `style.material.texture.path`
- `texturePositioning` lets shoppers drag, scale, and rotate the print; the result lands in `style.material.texture`
- `clearTexture` removes the print from the `shirt` binding group
- Add text or a logo in one click; it lands on the front and is selected for editing
- Ready-made spots (front chest, bottom, pocket, back) put a decal on the shirt without clicking the model
- Decals live on `binding.decals` in the shirt's own space: text with colour, font and weight, or an image (a sample logo or your own upload), edited live
- `decalMove` drags a decal across the fabric and returns the new surface point through `onCommit`; double-clicking a decal (`onDecalDoubleClick`) picks it up
- Size and rotation per decal
- Fabric colours are glTF material variants (`KHR_materials_variants`) on `sceneConfig.model.activeVariants`, switched from the page's own swatches
- The shirt is one mesh node, so a print or decal covers the whole garment
- The viewer download menu exports the shirt's editable UV layout as SVG

### `Before/After Comparison`

A kitchen before and after a renovation in `CompareViewer`:

- Slider and side-by-side layouts
- One camera for both panes, with hook commands that move both
- Keyboard-operable divider
- View-only rooms, with no inspection panels

The hosted demo compares a bedroom before and after a redesign:

- The "after" room is a BindingBuilder export: new colours, a wood floor, printed bedding and curtains, moved furniture and removed lights
- `semanticComparison` builds a change report with `compareViewerConfigurations`, delivered through `onComparisonChange`
- The report lists every recolored, retextured, moved or removed part
- Comparison highlights outline changed parts, toggled with `showComparisonOutlines` (off by default)
- Extra changes can be tried on the "after" room live, and the report updates
